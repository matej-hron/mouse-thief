# Pixel Quest: the world and the plan

The story bible of Pixel Quest (`adad.html`): the world, its people, the threat, the missions, and the rules that tie
them together. It is the place where new stories are written down and agreed on **before** they are built. Names of
people and places are fantasy names in both languages; the text of the game is Czech and English.

Status: a draft to iterate on. Nothing here is fixed until a mission is built.

---

## 1. The world: the Kingdom of Aldmere

A kingdom of rivers, forests and mountains, old and tired. For a long time nothing bad has happened, and most people
think nothing ever will.

| Region | What lives there | Kinds of stories |
|---|---|---|
| **Thornwall and the Riverlands** | villages, mills, the river, the old church on the hill | home base; first adventures |
| **Highcrown** | the royal city, the court, the Adventurers' Guild | intrigue, missions from the prince |
| **Elderwood** | elves, forest spirits, wolves, giant spiders | magic, riddles, winning the elves' trust |
| **Ironpeak** | dwarves, their lost mines, goblins and trolls | traps, machines, sieges, bargaining |
| **The Lantern Marsh** | will-o'-wisps, the drowned, an old abbey | creepy, puzzles |
| **Saltreach Coast** | a harbour town, pirates, a sea serpent | voyages, ships, treasure |
| **The Wild Steppe** | the Ash Riders, nomad clans | raids, chases, honour |
| **The Ashen Mountains** | ruins of an old empire, a dragon, Morgrath's tower | the threat's lair; the finale |

## 2. The threat: Morgrath, the Dark King

- **Who he is.** Once the court sorcerer of Aldmere, banished for forbidden magic. In the Ashen Mountains he found an
  old crown of shadow and named himself king. He wants the throne of Aldmere and to cover the land in darkness.
- **How he is shown.** A tall figure in a black crown with burning eyes, mostly in films and visions, rarely in person.
  Dangerous, not gory. Whether he is defeated for good or saved from the crown is decided in the finale (open).
- **How he works.** He rarely attacks openly. He causes trouble everywhere: curses (a valley in endless winter, crops
  that won't grow), kidnappings, stolen relics, spies at court, poisoned wells, monsters set loose, raids on villages.
  So almost any local trouble *might* be his doing, and sometimes it is.

### The Army of Darkness

| Part of the army | Who | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **The undead** | skeletons, the drowned, ghosts, wraiths | crypts, marshes, old battlefields |
| **Goblin tribes** | goblins, orcs, trolls, wolf riders | Ironpeak and the hills |
| **Beasts** | wargs, giant spiders, bats, giant rats, wyverns | Elderwood, caves |
| **People from the wild** | Ash Riders, bandits, the Red Coast pirates, cultists in red robes | the steppe, the roads, the coast |

### His lieutenants (each the boss of a story mission)

- **The Bone Abbot**: a necromancer in the Lantern Marsh who raises the dead. His cultists wear red robes. Brother
  Malrec (The Drowned Bell) was one of his tools, without knowing it.
- **Warlord Grishnak**: leads the goblins and trolls of Ironpeak; holds the dwarves' old mines.
- **The Wolf Mother**: turns the beasts of Elderwood against the people (and the elves).
- **The Raven Witch**: spy and curse-maker; her ravens are Morgrath's eyes everywhere, also at court.
- **Red Varga**: queen of the coast pirates and paymaster of the Ash Riders: people who serve Morgrath for gold.

## 3. Our side, and its troubles

- **King Aldric the Old**: tired and afraid. He wants peace at any price, so he would rather believe it is all
  rumours. Not evil, blind. The story can open his eyes at the end.
- **Prince Edric**: brave, and sees the truth. His father calls him a warmonger for it. He cannot act openly, so **he
  secretly sends the heroes on missions**: the quest-giver of the story missions, someone for the children to root for.
- **Advisers paid by Morgrath**: **Chancellor Vesk**, the main traitor, whispers to the king that "the prince wants a
  war". Others for side stories (a greedy treasurer...). Unmasking them is a thread of its own: evidence is collected
  along the way, and Vesk is confronted in front of the king.
- **Court wizard Orrin**: old, odd, on the prince's side, but locked in his tower (a story of its own).
- **Thornwall's innkeeper**: rumours and local jobs (the side missions start here).
- People from earlier stories come back: Mila, Reeve Hollis, Brother Malrec (if he was forgiven).

### Allies to win over

Nobody joins just because we are the good ones. Each has its own worries and pride, and that is where stories come from.

- **Noble houses**: some loyal, some wavering, some already bribed.
- **The elves of Elderwood**: they don't trust people; the king abandoned them once.
- **The dwarves of Ironpeak**: proud and quarrelsome; goblins hold their old mines.
- **Neutral oddballs**, each with its own price or puzzle: a troll under a bridge who wants to be called "sir", a
  riddling owl, the mushroom folk, a lonely giant, the will-o'-wisps of the marsh, the dragon of the Ashen Mountains.

Allies are collected like artefacts (a book of alliances); on the world map each won region changes colour. In the
finale an army of light meets the Army of Darkness, and who stands with you depends on whom you have won over (elven
archers, dwarven catapults, the giant...).

## 4. Missions

Two kinds, both on the world map:

- **Story missions** (a crown on the map): they move the big story forward. They start with a message from the prince,
  end with a short film of how the threat grows (or shrinks), give a **key artefact** and unlock the next ones.
- **Side missions** (a plain marker): local troubles. They stand on their own and give gold, experience, **bonus
  artefacts**, sometimes an **ally**, and sometimes a **clue** that the trouble was Morgrath's doing.

## 5. Rules

### Never start a mission you can't finish

- A mission unlocks by **level** and, for story missions, **key artefacts**. Never by bonus artefacts.
- **Key artefacts can't be missed**: every way of winning the mission gives it (beat the boss *or* talk them down).
- Inside a mission there are no dead ends: if the party falls they wake up and try again, and every obstacle has more
  than one way through (pick the lock, force it, or think).
- The mission's card on the world map shows exactly what is missing:
  *"The Bone Abbot · needs level 2 ✔ · needs the Prince's Signet ✖ (from The Prince's Messenger)"*. The START button
  appears only when everything is ✔.

### Artefacts

- **Key artefacts**: the reward for the main goal of a story mission; they unlock what comes next.
- **Bonus artefacts**: unique named magic items hidden in missions (a hidden room, a search, a riddle, a choice: e.g.
  Malrec's amulet only if he surrenders). Missable, never required. They make things easier.
- **One artefact per hero**: the party decides who carries what. It shows on the 3D figure (a glowing weapon...).
- **The artefact book**: a silhouette for each one, "The Drowned Bell: 2 of 3 found". Replaying a mission to find what
  was missed gives artefacts and gold, but less experience.
- Some bonus artefacts are also handy elsewhere (Saint Alder's hammer opens a dwarven door in Ironpeak: a shortcut,
  never the only way).

For The Drowned Bell (bringing back what was cut): **Saint Alder's Hammer** (a hidden ossuary under the crypt; hits
the undead much harder), **Shard of the Bell** (after the bell cracks; once per mission, ring it and the undead flee),
**Malrec's Amulet** (only if he surrenders; protects against Sleep and Hold).

### The party and levels

- One party for the whole campaign: the heroes keep their experience, gold, items and artefacts between missions.
- Levels 1 to 5: more HP, better hits, better saves, more spells, new spells (the cleric gets Hold Person, the mage
  Web...).
- Kind to children: nobody dies; a fallen party wakes up and tries again.

## 6. The maps

- **World map**: a small 3D model of Aldmere with its regions. Missions glow on it (crowns for story missions);
  locked ones show a lock and what they need. The party's figure walks along the roads. Morgrath's shadow spreads
  from the Ashen Mountains as the story goes on and draws back as lieutenants fall; won allies colour their regions.
  Tap a place: its card (title, level, what it needs, artefacts found), then START.
- **Mission map**: a parchment plan of the mission's scenes that fills in as you explore. Unexplored exits show as
  "?" (something may still be hidden there). It opens from a map button at the top; tapping a scene you have visited
  takes you back there.

## 7. The intro film

A short film (about a minute) before the first party is made, and again from the title (INTRO). It hints, it
doesn't tell everything:

1. **Aldmere at dawn**: rivers, hills, a village. *"Aldmere. A kingdom of rivers, forests and mountains."*
2. **The Ashen Mountains**: black peaks, lightning, a tower with one red window. *"But far in the east, in the Ashen
   Mountains, someone has woken."*
3. **The army**: rows of burning eyes in the dark, marching. *"His army gathers in the dark."*
4. **The throne room**: the old king on his throne, an adviser whispering in his ear. *"The old king doesn't want to
   hear it. His advisers whisper that all is well."*
5. **The prince**: Edric alone by a window, looking east. *"Only the young prince sees the danger. And he needs
   heroes."*
6. **Thornwall at night**: the church on the hill, the bell rings. *"Your story begins in Thornwall."*

## 8. The plan

1. **This document, and the intro film** (built in 0.3.1).
2. **The campaign frame** (built in 0.4.0; new spells for levels 3+ still to come): one party for the whole game, levels 1–5, the world map (Aldmere, with the Drowned Bell on
   it as the first side mission), the mission map, artefacts (the three of the Drowned Bell, with the hidden
   ossuary), the artefact book. The Drowned Bell gets its clue (the red robe).
3. **S1, The Prince's Messenger**: we write its scenes here first (like the Drowned Bell: 5–7 scenes, films, fights,
   choices), agree on them, then it is built.
4. **Then, in turns**: a side mission, a story mission, a side mission... each written here and agreed on first. The
   alliance book and the finale's army of light come with the first ally.
